## 1. Data Controller

This policy sets out how personal data collected through the Moonrose Clinic website (the "Website") is processed. It has been prepared by Dr. Salih Aygül as data controller under Turkish Law No. 6698 on the Protection of Personal Data ("KVKK").

## 2. Personal Data We Process

When you contact us through the WhatsApp and phone links on the Website, we process the following data you share with us:

- **Identity:** your first and last name.
- **Contact:** your phone number and e-mail address.
- **Security:** IP address and access logs kept by our hosting provider when you visit the Website.

The Website does not currently use cookies. If cookies are introduced, this policy will be updated and your consent will be requested.

## 3. Purposes of Processing

Your personal data is processed to:

- manage and follow up your appointment requests,
- answer your questions about our medical aesthetic services,
- keep the Website secure.

## 4. Transfer of Data

Your personal data may be shared with authorised public bodies to meet legal obligations. Otherwise it is not sold or transferred to third parties for commercial purposes. Messages you send via WhatsApp are also subject to WhatsApp's own privacy terms.

## 5. Your Rights (KVKK Article 11)

By applying to the data controller, you have the right to:

- learn whether your personal data is processed,
- request information about it if it has been processed,
- ask for incomplete or incorrect data to be corrected,
- ask for your data to be deleted or destroyed.
