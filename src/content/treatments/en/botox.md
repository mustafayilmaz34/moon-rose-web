## What is Botox?

Botox is a botulinum toxin treatment that temporarily reduces the movement of facial expression muscles. Repeated movements such as frowning, smiling or raising the eyebrows gradually create lines on the forehead, between the brows and around the eyes. When the muscle movement softens, these lines become less visible.

The aim is not to "freeze" the face. Applied in the right place at the right dose, your expressions are preserved and only the tired, tense look is eased.

## Which areas can be treated?

- Forehead lines
- Frown lines between the brows
- Lines at the corners of the eyes (crow's feet)
- Depending on the physician's assessment, medical indications such as jaw muscles, neck bands and excessive sweating

## How is it done?

Your facial expressions and muscle structure are assessed during the consultation first. The treatment consists of injections at a few points with very fine needles and takes about 10–15 minutes. Most people describe a brief pinprick sensation.

## Aftercare

- Avoid bending forward or lying down for the first 4 hours.
- Avoid massage, scrubs and peels on the area for the first 24 hours.
- The effect starts within a few days and settles in 10–14 days, when a follow-up visit is planned.

Duration varies from person to person but is about 4–6 months on average. Suitability, dose and frequency are decided after a physician's consultation.
