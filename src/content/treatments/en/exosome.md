## What is a skin booster?

"Gençlik aşısı" — literally "youth vaccine" in Turkish — is a common name for skin renewal treatments aimed at loss of hydration and vitality. Ingredients such as hyaluronic acid and amino acids are delivered into the middle layer of the skin to support its moisture balance and elasticity.

## What are exosomes?

Exosomes are tiny vesicles that play a role in communication between cells. In aesthetic dermatology they are used to support skin renewal. Exosome products can be applied by microneedling (dermapen) or with the mesotherapy technique. This is a new and developing field; suitability is assessed by the physician.

## Who is it for?

- Skin that looks dry, dull or tired
- Fine lines and loss of elasticity
- Acne scars or uneven skin texture (after a physician's assessment)

## The process

An anaesthetic cream is applied about 30 minutes before the treatment. The programme is usually planned as 3–4 sessions, 15–20 days apart. Increased hydration is generally noticed 7–10 days after the first session.

Brief redness and mild swelling may occur afterwards. The number of sessions and how long the effect lasts depend on your skin; the plan is made during the consultation.
