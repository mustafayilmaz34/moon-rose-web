## What are dermal fillers?

Age, weight changes and genetics can lead to volume loss in the face: cheeks flatten and lines deepen. Hyaluronic acid is a moisture-binding molecule that is also found naturally in the skin. Fillers based on it are used to support areas that have lost volume and to balance the contours of the face.

The goal is not a dramatic change, but a rested look that fits the natural proportions of your face.

## Which areas can be treated?

- Cheeks and cheekbones
- Lines running from the nose to the corners of the mouth
- Lip volume and lip borders
- Jawline and chin
- Under-eye hollows (depending on the physician's assessment)

## How is it done?

During the consultation your facial structure and expectations are discussed and the amount of product for each area is planned. After a local anaesthetic cream, the treatment is done with a fine needle or cannula and usually takes 30–60 minutes.

## Aftercare

- Mild swelling or bruising may last a few days.
- A cold compress is recommended for the first 24 hours; avoid pressing or massaging the area.
- Most people return to their daily routine the same day.

How long a filler lasts depends on the product, the area and each person's metabolism. The right product and amount are decided during the consultation.
