## What is mesotherapy?

Mesotherapy delivers very small doses of vitamins, minerals, amino acids and hyaluronic acid into the middle layer of the skin. The aim is to support the skin's moisture balance and vitality. It can be applied to the face, neck and backs of the hands.

## What is PRP?

PRP (platelet-rich plasma) is prepared from your own blood. A small amount of blood is processed, and the platelet-rich part is applied to the skin or scalp. Because it is made from your own blood, it contains no external substance. It can be applied with fine needles or by microneedling (dermapen).

## Who is it for?

- Skin that looks dull, tired or dehydrated
- Concerns about fine lines and skin texture
- Support for hair loss and hair density (after a physician's assessment)

## The process

Mesotherapy and PRP are usually planned as a series of sessions a few weeks apart. First changes are often noticed within 1–2 weeks; improvement in skin texture becomes clearer as the sessions progress.

Brief redness and mild swelling may occur at the treated area. The number of sessions and their spacing are decided during the consultation; how long the effect lasts varies from person to person.
