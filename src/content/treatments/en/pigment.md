## Why does pigmentation appear?

Dark spots usually appear when the skin produces too much melanin in one area. Sunlight, post-acne marks, hormonal changes (such as melasma) and age are the main causes. Each type of pigmentation has a different depth and responds differently, so no single method suits everyone.

## Which methods are used?

During the consultation the type and depth of the pigmentation are assessed and the plan is made accordingly. Methods may include:

- **Chemical peels:** controlled renewal of the upper layer of the skin.
- **Laser and carbon peel:** targeting pigment, usually as a course of several sessions.
- **Mesotherapy:** with ingredients that support an even skin tone.
- **Home care:** creams and serums recommended by the physician.

Intervals depend on the method — for example about 10 days for carbon peels and 3–4 weeks for laser treatments.

## Good to know

- Hormonal pigmentation such as melasma may not disappear completely; the aim is to lighten it and keep it under control.
- Spots can darken again with sun exposure. Reapplying a high-protection sunscreen every 2–3 hours and avoiding the midday sun, during and after treatment, is the most important step in keeping results.
- Some methods may cause redness and flaking for a few days.

The right method and number of sessions are decided during the consultation.
