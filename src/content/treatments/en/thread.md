## What is a thread lift?

A thread lift uses fine medical threads placed under the skin, which the body gradually dissolves. The threads gently reposition sagging tissue and also support the skin's own collagen production. It does not require surgery.

## When is it considered?

- Mild to moderate sagging of the cheeks and jawline
- Drooping of the outer eyebrow
- Loosening of the neck

During the consultation the physician assesses whether a thread lift is enough or whether surgical options should be discussed for more advanced sagging.

## How is it done?

The treatment is done under local anaesthesia. The threads are placed in planned directions with a fine needle or cannula. No incisions or stitches are needed.

## Aftercare

- Mild swelling, a feeling of tightness or bruising may occur in the first days.
- Avoid facial massage, exaggerated facial movements and sleeping face-down for a while.
- Most people return to their daily routine within a few days.

How long the effect lasts depends on the type of thread, skin structure and age. Suitability is decided during the consultation.
